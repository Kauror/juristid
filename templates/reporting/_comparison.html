{% comment %} One period measured against a comparable earlier one. Three things this card does deliberately. **It names both windows.** "+12" is meaningless without knowing that it is 1 January – 31 July against 1 January – 31 July. The dates are printed, not implied, because the single most misleading thing a year-on-year card can do is compare seven months with twelve (Stage-2E brief 33). **It stays neutral.** No green, no red, no arrow that has to be seen to be understood, no word suggesting the change is good or bad. More new matters arriving is more work the department was handed; more archived opinions is more letters, not more success. The reading is the reader's (brief 34, 51). **It refuses a percentage it cannot compute.** With nothing in the previous period there is no percentage change, and the card shows the absolute difference alone rather than ∞ or a fabricated 100 % (brief 73). {% endcomment %}
