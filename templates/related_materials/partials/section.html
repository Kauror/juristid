{% comment %} «Seotud materjalid» — the Matter as a small working dossier. Three things, in decreasing certainty (docs/adr/0062): 1. `Seotud teemad` — Matters a person confirmed as related. Canonical rows, rendered with the page, shown from both sides, no reasons: the decision is the source of truth now, not the score that once suggested it. 2. `Taustmaterjal` — earlier opinions and archive letters a person chose as useful background here. Also canonical, also immediate. 3. `Võimalikud seosed` — what the application *thinks* may be useful. Behind a control, computed only when opened, never counted on the ordinary page render, and every card says why it is there. Nothing here is mandatory, nothing here blocks anything, and a Matter with nothing confirmed and nothing opened is one quiet row. The whole section is what a decision re-renders (`hx-target="#seotud-materjalid"`), so the confirmed lists and the suggestions cannot disagree about the click that just happened. {% endcomment %}
