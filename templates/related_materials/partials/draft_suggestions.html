{% comment %} `Sarnased teemad` on `Uus teema` — earlier files that resemble what is being typed, before anything is saved. **The whole element, including its heading, is the swap target.** When the engine finds nothing the response is empty, so the heading goes with the cards and a form with nothing to suggest carries no empty box, no «midagi ei leitud» and no spinner. That is the one place this feature hides itself: the saved Matter's «Võimalikud seosed» keeps its deferred disclosure and its honest-emptiness message, because computing a count there would cost the computation the disclosure exists to defer (docs/adr/0062 §7, docs/adr/0087 §4). **Read-only, and it proposes nothing.** No `Lisa`, no `Ei ole seotud`, no form control of any kind: there is no Matter yet for a relation to attach to, and a suggestion that could write would be a second way to create one. A card is a title, why it is here, what Koda did there, and a link that opens it in a new tab — because the person is in the middle of filling in a form and taking them away from it would lose what they typed. The cards are deliberately the same shape and the same reason vocabulary the Matter page uses. A lawyer should recognise the answer, not learn a second one (docs/adr/0087 §2). {% endcomment %} {% if draft_suggestions %}
