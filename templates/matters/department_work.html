It is an oversight surface, not a staff evaluation. Every column is a count of observable states — how many files sit with somebody, how many carry a late instruction, how many carry none. There is no ranking, no score, no rate, no percentage and no colour grading of people, and the table is ordered alphabetically on purpose: sorting it by any of its numbers would turn it into a leaderboard, which the specification forbids (18.8) and which would make the underlying data worth gaming. A count of open matters is inventory. One can be a two-line monitoring note and the next a year of consultation, so it measures neither effort nor performance. Urgency is never carried by colour alone: every emphasised number sits under a heading that says what it counts, and every row of the rail states its reason in words. The wording rules are the handoff's — «läbi vaatamata», never «triaaž»; «Muutusteta 30 p», never «seisma jäänud». The page reuses Ülevaade's own shell — `page--overview`, `ovhead`, `seis`, `ovsection`, `railblock`, `railrow` — so the head reads two department surfaces that look like one product (design handoff, Osakond). {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
