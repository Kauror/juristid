Every other control in `LISA TEEMALE` and `TEEMA TOIMINGUD` saves where it stands, because every other control records something that can be corrected afterwards. This one cannot, so the person gets the three things a decision needs and nothing competing for their attention: **which** Matter, **what goes with it**, and **that it cannot be undone**. **The title is printed in full.** «Kas oled kindel?» over a button is a question nobody can answer — a lawyer with four tabs open has no way to tell which file the dialog belongs to. The Matter's own title is the answer, and it is the largest thing on the page. **The counts are the plan's, not a guess.** What is listed is what `build_deletion_plan` walked: the actual rows this deletion would remove, read off the ownership graph at the moment the page was rendered. It is rebuilt again under the Matter's row lock when the POST arrives, so what somebody agreed to is what happens — and a Matter that became undeletable in between is refused rather than half-deleted (`app.matters.deletion`). **A blocked Matter renders no button at all.** The refusal is the page. The form is simply absent, so there is nothing to press and nothing to be talked past; a crafted POST reaches the same refusal from the service, because the plan the service trusts is the one it builds itself. **`Loobu` is a link back to the Matter**, first in the source order and ordinary in appearance, because leaving is the safe answer and the safe answer should never be the harder one to hit. What this page deliberately does not do: no `confirm()`, no `hx-confirm`, no "type the title to continue". The first two are dismissible with a keystroke and say nothing; the third is ceremony that trains people to copy text without reading it (docs/adr/0096 §10). {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
