{% extends "base.html" %} {% block title %}Muuda teemat · {{ application_name }}{% endblock %} {% comment %} `Muuda teemat` — the whole record on one page, in the shape `Uus teema` asks for it. **`Uus teema` is the master.** Not "the two pages should look similar" — a direction, so that a disagreement between them has an answer. For every fact that exists on both, this page uses the master's label, the master's control, the master's vocabulary, the master's `Muu` rule and the master's order; and where a block of markup would otherwise exist twice, both pages include one partial. That is the correction the owner's live audit asked for, after a round in which the two pages had each been improved separately into two products (docs/adr/0096 §1). What is *not* shared is the business operation. Creating a Matter and correcting one are different transactions with different rules, and they keep their own form classes and their own services. Only the questions and the controls are the same. This page differs from the master in exactly three ways, and each is a consequence of the record already existing: - **values arrive pre-filled**, including vocabulary rows the department has since withdrawn, which are offered back and marked «kasutusest väljas» so that correcting a title cannot silently drop a filing; - **nothing is answered here that creation does not ask.** There used to be three: `Menetlusliik`, `Kellele` and `Sildid`, standing under a heading that said «ainult olemasoleva teema kohta». All three are gone from the ordinary Teema UI, and the heading with them. A question a lawyer never meets while filing, waiting on the screen they open to correct a mistake, is a question only ever answered by somebody guessing — and the page that is supposed to be the master's mirror had grown a section the master has no counterpart for. `Matter.track`, `Matter.addressee_organisation` and every stored `Tag` are untouched; what is gone is this page's claim to have an answer for them (docs/adr/0097 §2, §3, §4); - **`Märkmed` is not here.** The private scratch pad is written where the half-formed thought occurs — on `Uus teema` and on the Teema page's rail — and is `MatterPersonalNote`, scoped by author. It is not a Matter field and correcting a Matter is not the place to edit one. **`Nähtavus` is gone**, and its absence is the point rather than an omission: the ordinary Teema product does not ask who may see a Matter. There is no control, no hidden input and no field on the form, so a crafted POST to this route cannot move `Matter.visibility` either. Everything underneath it — stored visibility, child inheritance, restricted documents, authorization filtering — is untouched (docs/adr/0096 §3). **`Menetluse link` is here, from the same partial `Uus teema` draws.** It says where the proceeding this file is about is happening — a fact about the Matter in the same way its `Saatja` and its `Õigusakt` are — and it used to be asked under `LISA TEEMALE` on the Teema page, in a list of things that *happened*. It moved to where the Matter's other facts are answered, which means both pages that answer them, and the launcher chip and its route are gone rather than left reachable (docs/adr/0097 §5). And `Kustuta teema` stands at the bottom, apart from `Salvesta`, because the page a person opens to say «this record is wrong» is the page they open to say «this record should not exist» (docs/adr/0096 §4). {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
