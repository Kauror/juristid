That sameness is the point of this round. The two pages ask about the same record and were drawn as two different forms: chips on one, select boxes on the other, a different order, a different density. Two visual languages for one job is a page a person has to learn twice (02-EKRAANID §C). What is *not* shared is the business operation. Creating a Matter and correcting one are different transactions with different rules, and they keep their own form classes and their own services. Only the controls and the layout are the same. Two things this page has that `Uus teema` does not, because they only exist once a record does: Sildid and Nähtavus, and the compact `Muutumatu` strip that states the facts nobody edits. There is no «Järgmine tegevus» panel here. The next step belongs to the Teema page, where it is set beside the timeline it will appear on. {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
