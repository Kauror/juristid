{% extends "base.html" %} {% block title %}Muuda teemat · {{ application_name }}{% endblock %} {% comment %} `Muuda teemat` — the whole record on one page, in the shape `Uus teema` asks for it. **`Uus teema` is the master.** Not "the two pages should look similar" — a direction, so that a disagreement between them has an answer. For every fact that exists on both, this page uses the master's label, the master's control, the master's vocabulary, the master's `Muu` rule and the master's order; and where a block of markup would otherwise exist twice, both pages include one partial. That is the correction the owner's live audit asked for, after a round in which the two pages had each been improved separately into two products (docs/adr/0096 §1). What is *not* shared is the business operation. Creating a Matter and correcting one are different transactions with different rules, and they keep their own form classes and their own services. Only the questions and the controls are the same. This page differs from the master in exactly three ways, and each is a consequence of the record already existing: - **values arrive pre-filled**, including vocabulary rows the department has since withdrawn, which are offered back and marked «kasutusest väljas» so that correcting a title cannot silently drop a filing; - **three facts are answered here and nowhere else.** `Menetlusliik` and `Kellele` are deliberately absent from creation — one classification question per dimension, one counterparty question while filing (docs/adr/0090 §4, §5) — and `Sildid` are a working classification applied to a record that exists. They stand below the master's own questions, under a heading that says so; - **`Märkmed` is not here.** The private scratch pad is written where the half-formed thought occurs — on `Uus teema` and on the Teema page's rail — and is `MatterPersonalNote`, scoped by author. It is not a Matter field and correcting a Matter is not the place to edit one. **`Nähtavus` is gone**, and its absence is the point rather than an omission: the ordinary Teema product does not ask who may see a Matter. There is no control, no hidden input and no field on the form, so a crafted POST to this route cannot move `Matter.visibility` either. Everything underneath it — stored visibility, child inheritance, restricted documents, authorization filtering — is untouched (docs/adr/0096 §3). And `Kustuta teema` stands at the bottom, apart from `Salvesta`, because the page a person opens to say «this record is wrong» is the page they open to say «this record should not exist» (docs/adr/0096 §4). {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
