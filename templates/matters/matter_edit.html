That sameness is the point of this round. The two pages ask about the same record and were drawn as two different forms: chips on one, select boxes on the other, a different order, a different density. Two visual languages for one job is a page a person has to learn twice (02-EKRAANID §C). What is *not* shared is the business operation. Creating a Matter and correcting one are different transactions with different rules, and they keep their own form classes and their own services. Only the controls and the layout are the same. **On 2026-09-20 «the same shape» stopped being an aspiration and became the arrangement.** `Valdkonnad`, `Hetkeseis`, `Õigusakt` and `Menetluse link` are now four `{% templatetag openblock %} include {% templatetag closeblock %}`s of the partials `Uus teema` draws, so the two pages cannot disagree about a label, a chip, a reveal or a refusal — which they had begun to, in four separate small ways nobody had decided (docs/adr/0096 §2). **Four questions were deleted here**, and the page is shorter for it: `Sildid`, `Nähtavus`, `Menetlusliik` and the Matter-level `Adressaat`. None of them is hidden and none of the stored data is touched — `MatterEditForm` carries the reasoning per field, and `docs/adr/0096` §4 and §5 carry the decision. What remains that `Uus teema` has not is the compact `Muutumatu` strip, which states the facts nobody edits, and the assisted review. There is no «Järgmine tegevus» panel here. The next step belongs to the Teema page, where it is set beside the timeline it will appear on. {% endcomment %} {% block content %}

What a typed name *means* is still `resolve_source_organisations`: reuse an exact or alias match, create only a genuinely new body, refuse a spelling that names two. **`sender_row` is deliberately not passed, and that is §11.** It is what marks the row `bindAddresseeDefault` reads, and on `Uus teema` ticking a sender answers Adressaat because a file that arrived from X is normally answered to X. On an established Matter both are recorded business facts: adding a second sender to a Matter that already names its addressee must not silently rewrite the addressee, and there is no «ordinary case» to guess at because somebody already stated the answer. Without the attribute the script returns before binding anything, so the rule is absent here rather than suppressed (static/js/app.js `bindAddresseeDefault`, docs/adr/0069).
