{% comment %} Monday-first weeks, a week trigger in the first column. A day with records is a disclosure (`data-uxpopover`: one open at a time, Escape and a click outside close it, focus returns to the day); an empty day is plain text. The dots are inside the day's own button and are not controls themselves — the popover lists each record with its link, so nothing clickable is nested. The week's popover lists its days' records by date. Records known only to a period are never in it: they have no day in that week. Inside a day's popover — and under a day's heading in the week's — an entry does not repeat the day its heading already states (`in_day`); it keeps a date only where the record's own dates say more than that day, and always keeps a precision label and its state. The lists under the grid keep every date. `compact` is the department calendar: a work dot takes its responsible person's colour (`event.person_class`) and an entry is person — Teema, then what the date means. A rest day (UI-11) — Saturday, Sunday or an Estonian public holiday, from the one provider `app.core.holidays.rest_day` — draws its number in `--calendar-rest-day` (`is-rest`). A holiday is never told by colour alone: its name is in the day's `title` (hover), in its accessible name (keyboard) and in the popover heading. An otherwise empty holiday is a disclosure too, so a keyboard can reach it and a tap opens its name. Nothing is disabled or moved. {% endcomment %}
