{% comment %} One control for «which institution?», wherever `Uus teema` asks it. **What this replaces.** Saatja and Adressaat each used to offer three separate ways to name a body, and they had to be learned in order: a row of quick chips, a `
` reading «Vali nimekirjast (N)» holding a search box, and — a third control, somewhere else again — «Uus saatja» / «Uus adressaat» for a body the catalogue did not hold. Three paths to one answer, and the person had to decide which of them they were on before they could type anything. They are one path now: Otsi kõigepealt olemasolevat. Kui seda ei ole, lisa sama välja kaudu uus. The search box is the first control in the field, and the `+` attached to it takes whatever is in the box and says «this is a body you do not have». Nothing is folded away, and there is no second text input underneath (docs/adr/0073, task §2, §3, §12). **What did not change, and must not.** Typing is not creating. The search box posts nothing at all — it has no `name` — so a half-typed «Kliima» left behind after somebody chose `Kliimaministeerium` cannot become an institution. Only `+` writes, and what it writes is a *provisional* answer in the form's own state: `sender_name` / `addressee_name`, the fields that have always carried a typed institution. Nothing reaches the database until `Loo teema` runs, and what the name means there is still `app.organisations.services.resolve_organisation_name` — reuse an exact or alias match, create only a genuinely new body, refuse a spelling that names two (task §9, §10, §21). **One catalogue, two questions.** Saatja and Adressaat are the same `Organisation` rows reached through two relations, so this partial is included twice rather than written twice, and the ranking each asks for is the only difference in what it is given (`organisations_by_usage`, `addressees_by_usage`, docs/adr/0063). **The chips are the whole catalogue, and only the shortlist is on screen.** `tail` holds every institution the shortlist did not, rendered as real controls and `hidden` until the search finds one or somebody has already chosen it. That is what makes three separate things work without any of them having to reach a server: the search selects an existing row rather than describing one; a body chosen after a refused save comes back visible without the browser having to rebuild it; and the intake reader can fill Saatja with a ministry nobody put in the quick row, because the control it needs is already in the document (static/js/app.js `bindOrganisationPickers`, task §8, §16, §24). **Progressive enhancement, and the shape of the fallback.** Everything the scripted control adds is bound onto markup that posts correctly without it.
