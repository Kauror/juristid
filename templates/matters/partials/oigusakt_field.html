{% comment %} `Õigusakt` — what kind of instrument the file is about, on both pages that ask it. The third of the shared classification blocks, extracted for the reason the other two were: this markup already existed twice, and the two copies had already diverged — `Muuda teemat` put the free text in a row of its own below the fieldset while `Uus teema` revealed it inside, under the chips that open it (docs/adr/0096 §1).
