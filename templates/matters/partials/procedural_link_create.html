A Matter frequently arrives *from* a proceeding: an EIS notification, a ministry's covering letter naming its own register, a Commission consultation somebody was sent. The address is on screen at the moment the file is being opened, and making somebody create the Teema and then go and find it again is how it ends up in a browser history instead of on the file. **One box: the address.** It was a folded `
