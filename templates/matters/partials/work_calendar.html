{% comment %} Kalender — the rail's one calendar of this desk's dated work and recorded commencements (app/matters/calendar.py, docs/adr/0158 amendment of 2026-10-26). Read-only. Two categories, told apart by shape as well as colour: a round dot for *Tähtajad ja tegevused*, a diamond for *Jõustumised*. Each dot is one real record; a day shows three and «+N», and its popover lists every one. A day is the only place a dot goes. A record known only to a month, quarter, half-year or year is listed under the grid under its period — never on a day cell, never counted in a week (ADR 0079, ADR 0158 §4). The whole block is the HTMX target of its own links: each one is also an ordinary link to this page with the calendar's two parameters changed, so refresh, Back and a pasted address all show the same period (`hx-push-url`, and `historyCacheSize = 0` reloads it on Back). Quarter and year need more room than the rail has, so they open in a panel over the page. «Sulge» is a link to the month view, which is where Escape goes too. Inside the panel «4 nädalat» and «Kuu» stay in it (`kalendri_aken=1`, owner's review of 2026-10-30); their «Sulge» returns the same view to the rail. A click on a busy day in the panel lists that day under the calendars. On Osakond the same block is the department's calendar (`calendar.is_department`): its own element id, a work dot coloured by the person responsible for it, a legend of those people, and compact popover entries — person, Teema, what the date means — without the step's own sentence. {% endcomment %}
