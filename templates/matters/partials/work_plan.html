A step here has no date, no responsible person and no lateness; it is on no work list and in no count. It becomes work only when somebody starts it, and then it is the one step above. **State in words, never in colour alone.** Each row prints a marker and the word for its state — `Praegu`, `Tehtud`, `Soovitus`, `Plaanis` — so a suggested step reads as a suggestion to a screen reader as well as to the eye. A suggestion is quieter than real work and still readable text at full contrast: faint means a hollow marker, a lighter row and a small label, never a control that looks disabled. **Compact.** Skipped steps are out of the way here and restorable in `Muuda plaani`; there is no percentage, because a plan that may be skipped, repeated and extended has no honest denominator.
