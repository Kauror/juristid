Deliberately the same shape as `external_position_row.html` and `development_row.html`, because it is the same interaction on a sibling record. **Why this row had none until now.** `Arvamus välja` was the only chronology row with no correction control at all — `Meile saadetud tagasiside`, `Teiste arvamus`, `Kaasamine`, `Märge` and `Ülevaade / uudis` all carry one — on the record where a wrong date or a wrong recipient matters most: a letter registered with the day mistyped reported that date to the outbound register, the process rail and every report that counts advocacy, and the only repair was to ask an administrator (QA-023). **It corrects what we wrote down, never what happened.** The form asks four things — the day, the `Liik`, the `Kokkuvõte`, the addressees — and can reach nothing else. The evidence is immutable and is not on the form: a letter whose *text* was wrong is a new letter, which is what supersession is for. The status is not on the form either: un-sending, withdrawing and superseding are acts with their own meaning and their own audit events, and a spelling fix may not become one of them (`correct_sent_opinion`, docs/adr/0084 §8). **`Muuda` needs a writer, and does not need an open Teema.** Correcting what a closed file recorded about a letter it sent is the same kind of act as correcting an entry's text on one, and `withdraw_submission` already works on a closed Matter (docs/adr/0075 §12, docs/adr/0093 §3). The flag hides the button and decides nothing: the service takes the Matter's lock and makes every rule itself, because the browser that posts may be holding a page from before anything changed. {% endcomment %}
