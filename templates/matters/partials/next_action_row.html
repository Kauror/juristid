Three states carry three different meanings, and none of them relies on colour alone (Teema redesign §8): * **TEEN** is work Koda owes. Its date is a deadline and only this combination can be overdue. * **OOTAN** is a development expected from somebody else. Its date is when to check, and a passed one is not lateness. * **JÄLGIN** is a file to look at again later. A passed review date is a warning, never a danger. Lateness is the state of the row itself, and only a DO with a deadline can be in it.
