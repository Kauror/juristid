{% comment %} Imported OneNote files under an opened historical row of `Teema käik`: a note's directly attributed files, or every file of a document row. The caller supplies `files` (a sequence of `app.legacy_import.historical_notes.HistoricalFile`) and, optionally, `heading`. Each file reads as the record and the bytes it is: its title linked to the document page, its original filename where that differs from the title, the day the source says it was added to OneNote, the structural comment OneNote held beside it, and «lisatud enne praeguse lehe loomist» when its OneNote time is older than the page it is on now (the wording of `app.matters.timeline.ONENOTE_PREDATES_PAGE`). `Ava või laadi alla` is the exact bytes, named by the file so thirty of them are not thirty identical links. `added_display` is either a day («29.07.2026») or the source's own «lisamise kuupäev teadmata»; only a day gets «lisatud OneNote'i» in front of it, decided on the first character so no sentence here has to repeat the other module's wording. Read-only: no checkbox, no `Muuda`, no `Kustuta` — this is what the history held, not something to act on. {% endcomment %}
