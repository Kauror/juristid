{% comment %} `+ Kaasamine` — the three questions a consultation round actually has. **Who was engaged, when it happened, and what came back.** The panel used to open on a row of `Liik` chips and a four-way `Täpsus` control, so the first two decisions a lawyer made were a classification nothing ever read back and a precision an as-it-happens round never needs. Both are gone: every row written here is `Muu` and an exact day, historical rows keep whatever they were given, and correcting one is `Muuda` on its chronology row (docs/adr/0086 §1). **`Kaasamise kuupäev` starts filled with today and clears.** Recording something that just happened is the usual case, and the default is visible in the box where it can be read, changed and emptied — which is the one shape docs/adr/0078 §2 allows a date default to take. **`Tagasisidet ootame kuni` is asked here again — optional and empty** (docs/adr/0120, UQ-10). Without it the fact that decides whether a round is waiting could only be given by saving the round and then opening `Muuda` on it. What docs/adr/0091 §2 objected to was a *pre-filled* box that opened a wait nobody had decided on; this one has no default, so a round recorded as a completed act opens no wait, draws no work item and stores no deadline. Filled in, it is the same column `Muuda` corrects and the same wait `Ootan tagasisidet` opens on the row: the single `WorkItem`, `PRAEGUNE TEGEVUS`, «Tagasiside tähtaeg» on the rail and `Lõpeta kaasamine`, all as docs/adr/0086 §3 and §6 built them. Any day is accepted — past, today or future. {% endcomment %}
