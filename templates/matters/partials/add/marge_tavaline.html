It is the merge of two chips that used to stand side by side in the launcher: `+ Märge`, which took one sentence and wrote an `Entry`, and `+ Menetluse areng`, which took a classified date, two sentences, a stage and a next step and wrote a `MatterProceduralDevelopment`. **They were one question asked twice.** «Ministeerium saatis uue eelnõu versiooni» is a *menetluse areng*; «Rääkisin Justiitsministeeriumiga» is a *märge*; and a lawyer who has just done one of them is not thinking about which of the two words the application wants. `Menetluse areng` was never a phrase anybody asked for — it is an implementation abstraction that reached the screen — so it is gone from the screen, and what is left is the question: **mis juhtus?** (docs/adr/0097 §6). **One record type underneath, and it is the structured one.** `MatterProgressForm` carries the argument at length; the short version is that `MatterProceduralDevelopment` exists because a projection needs a title it did not have to parse, and that reason is untouched by anything in this round. `title` is «what happened», stated. `Entry.body` is prose. **Five controls, of which two are usually all anybody touches.** The date opens on today and clears; the sentence is required; the files, the stage and the next step are optional and, left alone, write nothing at all. **`Täpsus` is not here**, and neither is `Juristi märkus`. Both are deleted from this form rather than hidden — see `MatterProgressForm` for what each one cost and what still offers it (`Muuda` on the record, which decides per row). {% endcomment %}
