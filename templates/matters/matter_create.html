The page this replaces was right about its promises and wrong about its shape: nine full-width fieldsets stacked vertically, four of them holding a checkbox or radio row each, and roughly two thousand pixels between the title and the submit — with the procedural half and the next step behind two disclosures on top of that. Nothing was subordinate to anything, so it read as a survey rather than as "file this". What changed is density, not data. Every field that was here is still here, still posting the same name and the same value; both disclosures are gone and what they hid is on screen. The height came back from pairing rows and from the chip control, which is the same input with a smaller skin (design/mockups/Uus teema.dc.html 8a/8b/8c, Uus teema redesign §2, §3). Two things the shape still has to say, and one it must not: - the shape of a control is a promise about the data. Radios where the model holds one value — Vastutaja, Hetkeseis, Menetlusliik, Adressaat — checkboxes where it holds several. Chips are how they look; what they are has not moved (Stage-2E.1 brief 16, ADR 0025); - the two counterparty fields ask *different questions about one catalogue*, and they answer them the same way. Both may be chosen from the catalogue or typed; a typed name reuses an exact or alias match, otherwise creates the institution, and refuses a spelling that already names two — inside the same transaction that creates the Teema. `Saatja` was existing-organisations-only until docs/adr/0063 and is not any more: do not "restore" the rule that no institution is created here, because leaving a half-filled Teema to go and add a body under Asutused is not a workflow anybody used. What stays separate is what the two fields *mean* — `Matter.source_organisations` is who it came from and `Matter.addressee_organisation` is who it is answered to, one catalogue behind both (master specification 14.7, app/matters/services.py `resolve_source_organisations`); - only the title is required, and a page that shows a field is not a page that demands it (master specification 3.8). {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
