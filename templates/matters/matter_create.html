The page this replaces was right about its promises and wrong about its shape: nine full-width fieldsets stacked vertically, four of them holding a checkbox or radio row each, and roughly two thousand pixels between the title and the submit — with the procedural half and the next step behind two disclosures on top of that. Nothing was subordinate to anything, so it read as a survey rather than as "file this". What changed is density, not data. Every field that was here is still here, still posting the same name and the same value; both disclosures are gone and what they hid is on screen. The height came back from pairing rows and from the chip control, which is the same input with a smaller skin (design/mockups/Uus teema.dc.html 8a/8b/8c, Uus teema redesign §2, §3). Two things the shape still has to say, and one it must not: - the shape of a control is a promise about the data. Radios where the model holds one value — Vastutaja, Hetkeseis, Menetlusliik, Adressaat — checkboxes where it holds several. Chips are how they look; what they are has not moved (Stage-2E.1 brief 16, ADR 0025); - the two counterparty fields do *not* have the same contract, and the difference is deliberate. `Saatja` stays existing-organisations-only: its long tail is a filter over the catalogue and nothing on this page creates a sender. `Adressaat` may be chosen from the catalogue **or** typed — a typed name reuses an exact or alias match and otherwise creates the institution, inside the same transaction that creates the Teema. Do not "restore" the older rule that no institution is ever created here; it was replaced on purpose, because leaving a half-filled Teema to go and add a body under Asutused is not a workflow anybody uses (master specification 14.7, app/organisations/services.py `resolve_organisation_name`); - only the title is required, and a page that shows a field is not a page that demands it (master specification 3.8). {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
