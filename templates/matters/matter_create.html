The page this replaces was correct about the data and wrong about the shape: nine bordered boxes of equal weight, two seventeen- and twenty-five-item checkbox columns, and half the form behind `+ Täpsusta teema andmeid` and `+ Järgmine tegevus`. Measured at 1440 px with both disclosures open it was 2 139 px from the title to the submit, and four of its eight sections were 82 % of that. Nothing read as optional because nothing read as anything. Both disclosures are gone. Every field is on screen at load; the height comes back from pick rows, paired columns and a 1 px rule instead of a card. What is left of the hierarchy is carried by type: a 15 px title above the first rule, 11 px uppercase micro-labels, and one accent panel for the only block that is about the future rather than about the record. Nothing about the domain moved. Only the title is required (specification 3.8); radios where the model holds one value and checkboxes where it holds several; no organisation is created here; `Nähtavus` is still not on this page (design/UUS_TEEMA_HANDOFF.md). {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
