{% extends "base.html" %} {% load counts %} {% block title %}Kõik muudatused · {{ matter.title|truncatechars:60 }} · {{ application_name }}{% endblock %} {% comment %} `Kõik muudatused` — the technical change history, on its own page. **Why this exists.** `Teema käik` is a professional case history: canonical acts, business dates, evidence under the fact it evidences. Making it read that way means it does not show every write the application made — a corrected deadline, a renamed title, a field moved by one day. Those are real and they are auditable, and a primary history that lost them would have traded auditability for legibility. This page is the other half of that trade: the primary history stays clean *because* this page exists (docs/adr/0092 §11). **Read-only, and permission-safe by construction.** Every row comes through `app.audit.visibility.scope_change_events`, which is the one place that closes the gap between «this event hangs off a Matter you may read» and «this event is *about* a child you may read». A `Väline seisukoht` restricted below its Matter contributes no row here, exactly as it contributes none to the chronology — its existence, its type, its filename, its count and its date are all absent rather than redacted (AUTH-003, app/audit/visibility.py). **No payloads.** A `ChangeEvent.payload` carries identifiers and before/after scalars, and this page shows none of it: the row is when, who, what kind of change, and the summary the write itself recorded. A raw JSON blob on a lawyer-facing page is neither readable nor safely reviewable, and the payload is where a value the surrounding rules have not classified would first appear (docs/adr/0092 §11). **`SecurityAuditEvent` is not here and never will be.** Who opened and who downloaded what is a compliance record with its own retention and its own readers, not professional chronology (master specification 16.5). {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
