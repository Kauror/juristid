Adding these two questions to `+ Koja arvamus` would put two optional classification controls in front of the four required answers that panel exists to keep short, and adding them to the send would make classifying a letter a thing you can only do while asserting it went out. So this is one small screen that can change exactly two things and is structurally unable to change a title, a recipient, a date, a status or a file (docs/adr/0093, ADR 0061 §13). **The two controls are the ones `Muuda teemat` already uses**, chip checkboxes in a `chiprow`, because a person who has learned one classification control in this product has learned this one. What is deliberately *not* shared is the meaning: `Sildid` on that page say what the file is about, `Märksõnad` here say what this letter argued, and neither is copied into the other. **Nothing is pre-ticked from anywhere but this Submission's own assignments.** The Matter's tags are not offered as though they belonged to the letter, and no overview is selected because its address resembles the title or its date sits near the send. A blank form on a Matter full of tags and write-ups is the correct rendering and is what every existing opinion opens to (docs/adr/0093 §1, §2). {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
