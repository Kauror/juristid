{% comment %} `Võta tagasi` on a sent `Koja arvamus`, with its confirmation (docs/adr/0120, UQ-11). **One control, two places, one act.** The opinion's own row in `Teema käik` — where a person looking at the opinion finds it — and the opinion's ⋯ on Dokumendid both include this, and both post to `submissions:withdraw`, which is `withdraw_submission` and nothing else: the SENT → WITHDRAWN transition and its `SUBMISSION_WITHDRAWN` audit row. There is no second withdrawal path to keep in step. **Confirmed before it happens.** A withdrawal takes the letter out of every count of sent opinions and out of reporting, so the press that does it is the second one, under a sentence saying exactly that. A native disclosure, like `Kustuta` on a `Teema käik` row (`matters/partials/row_remove.html`): no second page, no `confirm()`, and `Loobu` closes it again. **Nothing is deleted, and the sentence says so.** The opinion stays in `Teema käik` as sent on its day, with «Arvamus tagasi võetud» under it, and the file that went out stays its evidence (docs/adr/0102 §1, docs/adr/0092 §3). The caller supplies `submission`, and `withdraw_back` — `"teema"` from the Teema page, which the route answers by returning there; empty elsewhere, where it lands on Dokumendid as it always has. On the Teema row the chip is named by reference to the row's headline (`koja-arvamus--pealkiri`, the element `Muuda` beside it is named by), so a file with three opinions has three distinguishable `Võta tagasi` controls. {% endcomment %}
