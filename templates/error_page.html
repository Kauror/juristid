{% load static %} {% comment %} The page Django itself renders when something went wrong before, or instead of, a view: a 404, a 500 and a refused CSRF check (templates/404.html, 500.html, 403_csrf.html). **Standalone, and on purpose.** A 500 is rendered with no request and no context processors, often because something those depend on is what failed, so nothing here may need either: no navigation, no persona, no counts, no `{% url %}` beyond what cannot fail. Django's fallbacks were English pages on an Estonian application (ENG-012). 403.html and 405.html joined the family after the 2026-10-17 audit (JUX-013). **Its own gutter.** Without the application's shell nothing else spaces it: `body` has no margin and `.page` no padding, so on a phone the text touched the window's edge (JUX-014). `.page--standalone` gives it the page's gutter. {% endcomment %} {# The browser's chosen theme, before anything is painted (static/js/theme.js). #} {% block title %}{% endblock %} · Koda Õigusloome
