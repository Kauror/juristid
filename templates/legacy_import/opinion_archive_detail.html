The retired proposals are last and are shown rather than hidden — an audit of the migration needs "we used to believe this, and here is what replaced it" far more than it needs a tidy page. The link form says what it does not do, in the form itself. Tying a file to a matter is a weaker claim than a canonical opinion, and a reviewer who thinks they have just recorded a sent opinion has been misled by the interface. **Every Matter on this page comes through `MatterView`.** Reading the archive is a decision about the corpus; naming a register entry is a decision about that entry, and it is not the archive's to make. Where the reader may not read the entry, the view carries no object at all — so a relationship is acknowledged as existing and nothing about it is rendered. That is deliberately not the same as saying there is none: the search projection records the letter as filed, and a page claiming otherwise would be wrong rather than discreet (docs/adr/0027). {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
