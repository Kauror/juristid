{% comment %} The Matter's structured facts, as one swappable block. The wrapper exists so `app.intelligence` can answer an add with the section the record landed in, rather than with a redirect: `#teema-faktid` is what the inline forms target, and re-rendering it closes whichever form was open, shows the new row, and leaves the reader where they were. This app renders its own block from its own selector and its own authorization helpers. It does not reach into `app.matters`' context builders, which is the coupling ADR 0018 refused and this still refuses — what ADR 0065 changed is that a fact section may re-render *itself* (docs/adr/0065). `inline_fact` is deliberately absent from the Matter view's context. Rendered from there the variable resolves to nothing, every `{% if %}` below is false and the block is the closed state — which is what the Matter page always wants, and is one intelligence-specific key that does not have to travel through `app.matters.views` to say so. {% endcomment %}
