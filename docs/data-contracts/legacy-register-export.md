# «Laadi Excel vana Exceli formaadis» — legacy register export

Status: implemented (`app/reporting/legacy_workbook.py`), owner Decision A.
Route: `reporting:export_legacy_xlsx`, `/statistika/eksport/vana-excel.xlsx`.

An outbound data contract: what the downloadable workbook contains, where each
column comes from, and what it deliberately does **not** promise.

## What it is — and what it is not

A **unified 2026-style** export of the register. Every data sheet carries the
reviewed 2026 era contract's columns A–L with its exact headers, read at run time
from [`excel-era-2026.toml`](excel-era-2026.toml) (never retyped, so a contract
correction moves the export with it).

It is **not** an era-faithful reproduction of the historical «Tööd eelnõudega»
workbooks. Those had five column eras — 2011–2017, 2018–2019, 2020–2022,
2023–2024, 2025–2026 — with different columns, header rows and, before 2020, a
G column that meant the *sender*. **Round-trip compatibility is not claimed**:
the file cannot be re-imported into Juristid, and a sheet here is not
interchangeable with the original sheet of the same year.

## Workbook layout

| Sheet | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `Teave` (first) | Generated at (Tallinn time), generated by (display name), scope, and every caveat below, plus the row count per sheet. |
| `2026`, `2025`, … (newest first) | One sheet per **register year**. Only years that have rows. |
| `Teadmata` (last, always present) | Teemad with no register year. |

**Register year** is `Matter.reporting_year`, but only for the origins in
`REGISTER_YEAR_ORIGINS`; everything else — no reporting year, or a
`LEGACY_ONENOTE` record — goes to `Teadmata`. The split is computed with
`app.matters.selectors.unknown_register_year_q` (the exact complement of
`register_year_q`), never from `created_at`.

**Row order** inside a sheet: reference ascending (`reference_year`, then
`reference_number`, both nulls last), then title, then id — deterministic.

**Population**: `Matter.objects.visible_to(user).real_data()` — the same
population the Statistika CSV exports start from (`reporting_population`): every
Teema the reader may see, open, closed and archive; real data only (TEST records
excluded); deleted Teemad excluded by the default manager. No period or other
filter. An account with no business read (administrator without a grant, the
shared-gate session without a persona, anonymous) gets `Teave` and an empty
`Teadmata`; the route itself redirects anonymous and persona-less sessions to
sign-in / the persona chooser.

### Where «the imported register» comes from

1. `CurrentRegisterState` (the reviewed latest-snapshot interpretation) when the
   Teema has one; the original VÄLJA text is read from its source reference via
   `source_cell`.
2. Otherwise the latest `MatterSourceReference` read under a reviewed era
   contract (newest contract year, then lowest row number), excluding rows a
   reviewer left in conflict (`CONFLICTING_EVIDENCE`, `UNRESOLVED`). OneNote and
   Lovable provenance rows have no era contract and never answer.

## Judgment calls

- **VÄLJA mixes two kinds of value on purpose, and the cell type tells them
  apart.** A real date is a recorded send in Juristid; text is the historical
  register record. A register date is printed as *text* so it can never be read
  as a send (ADR 0011: a date is not evidence). «ei saatnud» is never turned into
  a date or a send.
- **Child visibility.** Submissions and NextActions are read through their own
  `visible_to`, so a RESTRICTED opinion or step on a readable Teema does not
  contribute its date, addressee or text for a reader who may not see it. (Since
  docs/adr/0181 every business reader sees RESTRICTED children; the rule still
  holds for any narrower scope.)
- **JÄRGMISEKS is canonical only.** The register row's date fallback chain is a
  *date* display, not text, and the imported `next_action_text` is a source
  instruction, not a step — neither is written here.
- **HETKESEIS/JÄRGMISEKS are current values.** On a 2014 sheet they say where
  the Teema stands today, not in 2014.
- **G is always KELLELE.** The pre-2020 KELLELT (sender) is not in this file; it
  remains in Juristid's provenance and in the comprehensive export.

## What is lossy

- Era-specific columns that do not exist in the 2026 layout (and the pre-2020
  KELLELT) are dropped.
- Multiple addressees collapse into one `"; "`-joined cell.
- Historical HETKESEIS/JÄRGMISEKS texts are replaced by current values.
- Register VÄLJA marks are text, not dates; approximate or non-date marks keep
  their words.
- Sender organisations, provenance, documents, timelines and every other
  Juristid record are absent.

## Cost

Six queries regardless of size: the Matters (with owner, stage, addressee,
register state and its source row joined), their legal instruments, the sent
Submissions, those Submissions' addressees, the open steps, and the fallback
source rows. Measured on 2,000 synthetic Teemad across 16 years (local
PostgreSQL 18, Windows dev box): ~2.3 s wall, ~19 MiB peak traced Python
memory; most of the time is openpyxl cell styling in the write-only writer.
The whole row set is held in memory before writing (so `Teave` can carry the
counts); that is linear and small at register scale.
